Module for base64 encoding and decoding.

Implemented natively by the runner (Rust base64 crate) — sources never have to carry their own decoder, which keeps the wasm smaller and the decode faster. The runner tolerates all four common variants (standard padded/unpadded and URL-safe padded/unpadded), matching what real grab pages ship.
